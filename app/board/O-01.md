# Login first, then onboarding (product decision)
status: todo
deps: A1 state cleanup
files: app/src/auth/*, app/src/shell/*, app/src/screens (account, welcome, discover, reveal, save), app/tests

## Decision (user, overrides journey-spec §12 "glimpse before account")
Every user signs in first. Welcome, What brought you here, Discover and the reveal are the post-login onboarding. Nothing in the app is public except the sign-in screens.

## Flow
1. Sign in (one screen for new and returning users, reusing the original's account/code screen design): email, then 6-digit code. Tenant users can use SSO ("Sign in with your organisation") or arrive by invite. Passkey sign-in for returning users who added one.
2. New account only: first name and the consent boxes (unticked; required = privacy notice acknowledgement). Mobile is dropped until SMS verification exists (GDPR minimisation).
3. Onboarding (new users, or anyone who has not finished it): Welcome (value promise, "Let's start"), What brought you here (optional), 6 Discover questions, money-personality reveal, then straight into Home with "Make my plan". The "Save your results / Skip for now" screen (DS) is removed: there is an account already.
4. Returning users land where they left off: unfinished onboarding resumes at the next unanswered step; finished users land on Home. Progress is saved to the server, so it resumes on any device.
5. Sign out returns to the sign-in screen.

## Remove (less code)
- The public local-only flow and the pre-login carry-over of Discover answers (B12 carry-over, local answer store, TEST_AUTH special cases that only existed for it).
- The DS save/skip screen and its copy.
- Wall exceptions for D0-D7/DR/DS.

## Done when
- Signed out, any URL lands on sign-in; after sign-in a new user starts at Welcome, a returning user resumes or lands on Home.
- Onboarding answers are saved through the plan API and survive reload and a second device (second browser context).
- Pixel check: Welcome, Discover and reveal screens unchanged; sign-in uses the original's account/code styling; DS removed from tools/visual-screens.json with a note.
- tsc, tests and `npm run size` lower than before.
