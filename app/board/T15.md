status: todo
deps: T5

## Goal
whatIf(plan, {extraMonthly, oneOff, goal}) returns the new covered %, the live line figures ('you'd save EUR 1,725 a month (1,000 + 725 extra) / spare money about X') and an over-spare warning flag (sec 24.2, 26). scenarios(plan) re-runs the engine one change at a time then combined rows (retire at 60/65, save N more with N ~ 18% of take-home, pension at max relief, full State Pension, pay N more into pension, combined paths), skipping rows that change nothing; each row has covered % and the age the gap starts (sec 28).
