# LifeMap GDPR audit

Date: 28 Oct 2026. Scope: `api/` (Hono, Better Auth email OTP + passkeys, Drizzle schema, plan API, consents, guard, Lambda entry), `app/` (React PWA rewrite: `src/auth`, `src/state`, screens, sheets, report, `vite.config.ts`), the legacy static site (`sw.js`, `_site/`, `app/public/prototype.js`), `docs/journey-spec.md` and `docs/pre-release-verify.md`. Read-only: no product code changed. The API is being moved to the Serverless Framework (eu-west-1) at the same time, so infrastructure findings are against `sst.config.ts` and `api/src/lambda.ts` as they stood on 28 Oct 2026. Recheck them against the new `serverless.yml`.

Severity: **blocker** = must not take real customer data until fixed. **high** = fix before launch. **medium** = fix soon after launch, or before scale. **low** = hygiene.
Owner: **code** = developers. **legal** = lawyer or DPO. **ops** = infrastructure and process.
"Unverified" means the repo does not show it either way. Someone has to check it on the deployed system.

## Verdict

**Not ready for real customers.** It is fine with synthetic test data only.

What is good already:
- The region is eu-west-1 (`sst.config.ts:10`).
- The API denies by default (`api/src/guard.ts:6-14`).
- OTP codes are never logged in production, because the sender fails closed (`api/src/app.ts:7-13`).
- Plan database errors log only the error code, never the plan (`api/src/routes/plan.ts:24-29`).
- Better Auth's default cookies are HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax, and Secure on https.
- Consent boxes start unticked (`app/src/state/fresh.ts:34-35`).
- Foreign keys with `ON DELETE CASCADE` hang every per-user table off `user` (`api/src/db/schema.ts:25,33,56,70,79`), so a real erasure is a small job.
- The React app keeps the plan in memory, not in localStorage.

What blocks launch:
1. "Delete my data" deletes nothing on the server. It also tells the user it did.
2. Withdrawing marketing consent is never recorded. The original grant stays in the database.
3. There is no privacy notice and no DPIA.
4. "Download my data" points at an endpoint that does not exist.
5. Retention is not defined anywhere.
6. Adviser sharing has no consent record or access model.
7. The database, its encryption and its backups are not defined in the infrastructure code.

## Must fix before real customers

1. **G-01** A real `DELETE /api/me`: the user row and its cascade, the verification rows, documents, shares, client cache and session. Plus the backup suppression list. Show "deleted" only on success.
2. **G-02 / G-03** An append-only, versioned consent log in which withdrawal is recorded and respected. Consent writes that do not fail silently.
3. **G-04 / G-05** A privacy notice and terms. The "required" tick becomes an acknowledgement of the notice version, with the basis set to contract.
4. **G-06** Remove or fix every untrue sentence: "we don't save anything", "check your inbox", "invite sent", "deleted after reading", "all your data was deleted".
5. **G-07** A working `GET /api/me/export`, plus `navigateFallbackDenylist` for `/api`.
6. **G-08** Retention periods, a purge job (verification, sessions, inactive accounts), hashed OTPs, and log retention.
7. **G-13** A database in IaC: encrypted, TLS enforced, private, with a defined backup window.
8. **G-14 / G-15 / G-16** Shared OTP rate limiting with a per-email limit, a single https origin with `BETTER_AUTH_URL` and trusted origins set, and an OTP sender chosen by an explicit flag.
9. **G-17** Self-host the fonts in the app and in the report.
10. **G-09** Stop requiring the mobile number, or stop marking it verified.
11. **G-23 / G-24 / G-25** DPIA, ROPA and processor DPAs, and a breach runbook (legal and ops, but they gate launch).
12. **G-12** Adviser sharing: consent records and access model. This gates the booking feature, not sign-up.

## Needs a lawyer or DPO, not code

- Lawful basis for each data category and purpose (G-04). In particular, whether adviser sharing rests on consent or contract, and whether reminders count as service messages or marketing.
- Whether LifeMap is the controller, and whether adviser firms are independent controllers or processors. Draft the data-sharing agreement or DPA (G-12).
- The DPIA, and whether residual risk needs prior consultation with the DPC (G-23).
- Privacy notice and terms wording, including the profiling explanation, the referral fee and conflict disclosure, and how notice versions are managed (G-05, G-11).
- Whether the risk profile and personality engage Art. 22 or MiFID / CBI suitability rules. Whether adviser-only behaviour tags are fair to keep (G-11).
- Retention periods. This includes any CBI record-keeping duty, for example under the Consumer Protection Code, if LifeMap counts as a regulated intermediary (G-08).
- Partner and joint-plan rules, and Art. 14 notices to partners (G-21).
- The ROPA, processor due diligence and transfer assessments (Neon, Google, an LLM provider) (G-24).
- Breach notification thresholds and who decides (G-25).
- Whether a DPO must be appointed (Art. 37: large-scale regular monitoring through profiling makes it likely).
- Whether the CBI Consumer Protection Regulations 2025 ban on pre-ticked boxes is met by the acknowledgement design in G-04.

## Not verified

- Any deployed configuration: the Serverless migration was in progress and no `serverless.yml` existed at audit time.
- The database host and its encryption, TLS and backups.
- CloudFront or any front-end hosting, headers and access logs.
- Whether the legacy `_site` was ever served on the production origin (G-18).
- Whether the Workbox navigation fallback intercepts `/api/me/export` (G-07).
- What Better Auth's logger writes on errors (G-16).
- Whether a future "Ask" chat or document extractor sends data outside the EU.
