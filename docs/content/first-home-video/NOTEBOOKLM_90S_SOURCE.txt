FACT SHEET (use only these facts)
- First-time buyers can usually borrow up to 4x gross income, with a minimum 10% deposit (Central Bank of Ireland rules).
- Help to Buy: refund of income tax and DIRT paid, up to 30,000 euro, on new builds and self-builds.
- First Home Scheme: shared equity from the State and banks, up to 30% on new homes, within price limits.
- Local Authority Home Loan: government-backed mortgage when a bank refuses.
- Extra costs: stamp duty (1% up to 1 million euro), legal fees, valuation and survey, mortgage protection and home insurance.
- Approval in principle usually lasts about 6 months. Lenders check savings, credit record (Central Credit Register) and debts.
- Mortgage types: fixed, variable, green (lower rates for efficient homes).
