Buying your first home in Ireland? Stop. Watch this before you sign anything.
One. You can borrow up to four times your income, with a ten percent deposit. But choose a repayment you can sleep on.
Two. Add stamp duty, legal fees and a survey to your budget.
Three. Claim every benefit: Help to Buy, and the First Home Scheme.
Four. Fixed gives certainty, variable can move.
Five. Get approval in principle before you view a single house.
Six. Protect your family with mortgage protection.
Do this, and eight months later, you're holding the keys. Time to put the kettle on.
