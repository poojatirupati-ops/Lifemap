## 5. FACT SHEET (use these facts; confirm current rules before publishing)
- **Central Bank mortgage measures:** first-time buyers can usually borrow up to 4x gross annual income, and need a minimum 10% deposit (90% loan-to-value). Lenders have limited allowances above these limits. Rules can change.
- **Help to Buy:** refund of income tax and DIRT paid over the previous 4 years, up to a cap of 30,000 euro or 10% of the price (whichever is lowest), for new builds and self-builds.
- **First Home Scheme:** shared equity from the State and participating banks, up to 30% for new homes and up to 20% for second-hand, within regional price ceilings. Can be combined with Help to Buy in some cases, but the State's share may be capped lower. No service charges for the first five years.
- **Local Authority Home Loan:** government-backed mortgage for people refused by lenders. Income and property price limits apply.
- **Vacant property grants:** grants exist for refurbishing vacant or derelict homes.
- **Stamp duty:** 1% on homes up to 1 million euro, higher above that. Sources conflict on any first-time buyer relief, so say only 1%.
- **Other costs:** legal fees, valuation, survey, mortgage protection insurance (required by lenders), home insurance, Land Registry fees.
- **Approval in principle:** shows your budget before you view homes, usually valid for about 6 months.
- **Lenders check:** savings pattern, Central Credit Register record, debts and spending.
- **Mortgage types:** fixed (rate set for a period), variable (can change), green (lower rates for energy-efficient BER A/B homes), term (longer term = lower payment, more total interest).
- **BER rating:** energy label from A (best) to G; affects running costs.

## 6. COMMON FIRST-TIME BUYER FAQs (extra source material)
1. **How much deposit do I need?** Usually 10% of the price, plus several thousand euro for fees.
2. **How much can I borrow?** Generally up to 4x gross income, but lenders also check your outgoings.
3. **Can I use Help to Buy and the First Home Scheme together?** In some cases on a new build; check eligibility and caps.
4. **What if I'm refused by a bank?** Ask about the Local Authority Home Loan.
5. **Fixed or variable?** Fixed gives certainty; variable can rise or fall. Think about what you can afford if rates rise.
6. **How long does approval in principle last?** Usually about 6 months.
7. **What happens if rates rise 1%?** Your repayment goes up; test your budget before you commit.
8. **Do I need a solicitor and a survey?** Yes, a solicitor handles contracts; a survey checks the property's condition.
9. **Do I need mortgage protection?** Yes, lenders require life cover for the mortgage.
10. **Where do I get help?** A verified mortgage expert, such as the experts matched on LifeGoals.
